Privacy Policy · Effective October 2026 · Contact: furfile.app@gmail.com（示例，上线前替换为真实邮箱）

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in FurFile — pet profiles, schedules, weights, health records, and photos — is stored only in a local database on your device. This version of the app makes no network requests: no uploading, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. All features work fully offline.

Backups & migration

"Export backup" creates a JSON file on your device — you control where it goes. "Restore" only reads a file you explicitly choose. Backup files contain all of your records; keep them safe.

Photo & camera access

The photo library/camera is accessed only when you actively add a photo. Photos are compressed and stored locally on your device only.

Notifications

Reminders (3 days before / due day / overdue) are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device. No push servers are involved.

Health information disclaimer

Vaccine and deworming schedules in FurFile are structured presentations of publicly available vaccination guidelines, for record-keeping reference only. They do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Consult a veterinarian with any questions.

Future cloud sync

An optional cloud sync may be introduced in a future version. It will be off by default and require explicit opt-in, with a clear explanation of where data goes before you enable it. Until then, this policy stands as written.

Children

FurFile is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects your data, the in-app privacy page will be updated in the same release. If anything here ever disagrees with what the app actually does, the app's actual behavior governs — and we consider that a bug worth reporting.

数据存在哪里

你在毛孩手帐录入的一切数据（宠物档案、日程、体重、就诊记录、照片）默认仅保存在你设备的本地数据库中。当前版本不发起任何网络请求：不上传、无分析、无广告、无第三方 SDK，断网可使用全部功能。

备份与迁移

「导出备份」在设备上生成 JSON 备份文件，去向由你掌握；「从备份恢复」只读取你主动选择的文件。备份文件包含全部档案数据，请妥善保管。

照片与相机权限

仅在你主动添加照片时访问相册/相机，照片压缩后仅存于本机。

通知

本地提醒（提前 3 天 / 当天 / 逾期）完全在你的设备上排程与触发，不依赖任何推送服务器。

健康信息声明

应用内疫苗/驱虫日程来自公开免疫程序模板的结构化整理，仅供记录参考，不构成医疗建议、诊断或用药建议。如有疑问请咨询兽医。

未来的云同步

未来版本可能提供可选云同步：默认关闭、显式开启，开启前会明确告知数据去向。在此之前本政策所述即为全部实际行为。

未成年人

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人数据。

政策更新

如政策发生影响你数据的变更，将随应用版本同步更新应用内隐私页。若本页与应用实际行为不符，以实际行为为准，并欢迎反馈。
